Spield earns from two places, both capped by the contracts themselves. Everything else in the
protocol is fee-free. This page lists exactly what is charged and what is not, so there are no
surprises.

## At a glance

| Fee | Charged on | Rate | Hard ceiling in the contract |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Yield fee** | YT interest, at the moment it is paid out | **5%** | **10%** |
| **Swap fee** | Each PT or YT trade in the market | Scales with time to maturity | 5% a year on the fee root |
| **Protocol share of the swap fee** | The swap fee above | **20%** (LPs keep **80%**) | **50%** |

The ceilings are enforced on-chain, so even a compromised admin key could not push a fee higher.

## 1. The yield fee: 5% of YT interest

YT holders earn the interest the position produces. When that interest is **paid out** (by a
claim, by the Router's "claim to USDC", or when the Fixed-Rate Vault collects its YT yield),
**5% of it goes to the protocol** and 95% to the holder. The mainnet Series 2 yield contract
was verified at 500 bps (5%).

```text
interest paid out  =  holder 95%  +  protocol 5%
```

- It is charged on **interest only**. Never on principal, never on PT.
- It is charged **when interest is paid**, not while it accrues. Selling or transferring YT
  credits the interest you earned *before* the sale to you, in full, and the fee applies when
  you claim it.
- The Fixed-Rate Vault's coupon is funded from YT interest, so the vault's rate is checked
  **net of this fee** before launch. See [the yield source](/docs/how-it-works/yield-source).
- The ceiling is **10%** (1,000 bps). The fee can be changed only within it.

This is the main revenue line. It scales with how much money is in the protocol, not with trading
volume. The Yield page in the app shows the fee in **Protocol yield fee**.

## 2. The swap fee, split between LPs and the protocol

Every PT or YT trade pays a fee that is built into the price:

```text
fee rate = exp( ln_fee_root x years to maturity )
```

It is larger far from maturity and falls to nothing at maturity, so it stays a roughly constant
share of the *yield being traded* at every term. The launch configuration uses a root of
0.25% a year, with an on-chain ceiling of 5% a year. See
[the yield market](/docs/features/yield-market#the-fee-scales-with-time-to-maturity).

That fee is then **split**:

| Share | Goes to | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **LPs** | Stays in the pool and raises every LP share's value | **80%** |
| **Protocol** | Paid out of the pool to the treasury, in SR | **20%** |

- The protocol's share has a hard ceiling of **50%**, so LPs can never be pushed below half the fee.
- **PT trades and YT trades pay the same split**, so no route is cheaper than another.
- The protocol share is deliberately **lower than the LP share**.
- Swap fees are a modest line next to the yield fee. They mainly compensate liquidity providers.

Treasury (USTRY) series markets use the same split model. Fee changes on those markets are
**scheduled and apply after a delay of at least 24 hours**, so traders and LPs see a change
coming.

## What is not charged

- **No fee to wrap or unwrap SR.** SR moves 1:1 with its share of the yield source.
- **No fee on the Router.** It is a convenience layer. Network fees and the market's swap fee
  still apply to the route's underlying trade.
- **No fee to deposit into the Fixed-Rate Vault**, beyond the yield fee on the interest the
  vault earns.
- **No fee to redeem PT** at maturity, and no fee to recombine PT and YT.
- **No fee to add or remove liquidity.**
- **No yield fee in a Treasury (USTRY) series.** Its fees come only from its market's swap fee.
- **Network and bridge fees are separate.** Stellar network fees apply to every transaction, and
  cross-chain transfers pay Circle's transfer fee shown in the quote.

## Where the money goes

Protocol fees are paid **in SR** to a **treasury address** stored on each contract. You can read
it on-chain (`treasury()`), and the market keeps a running `treasury_earned` total, so the flow is
auditable. Fee settings and the treasury address are changed by the contracts' admin, which on
mainnet is a **2-of-3 multisig**. See [Security](/docs/resources/security).

<Callout title="Surplus after maturity is not revenue">
  After a series matures, the SR held above PT cover is **owed to YT holders**, not the protocol.
  Only money that is provably owed to nobody (rounding dust and YT interest abandoned by burning
  YT without claiming) can ever be swept to the treasury, and a healthy series sweeps close to
  nothing. See [Solvency](/docs/how-it-works/solvency#after-expiry).
</Callout>
