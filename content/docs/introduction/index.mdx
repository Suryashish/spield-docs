**Spield is the fixed-income layer for Stellar.** It takes a yield-bearing position,
such as USDC earning interest on Stellar's lending market, and splits it into two simple,
tradable pieces:

- a **Principal Token (PT)**, a *fixed-rate bond*. It redeems **1:1 for your principal at a set date**, and you buy it today at a small discount. That discount *is* your fixed return.
- a **Yield Token (YT)**, a *claim on all the yield* that position earns until that date. It is a cheaper, leveraged way to bet that yield will be high.

Together, PT and YT always add up to the original position. Nothing is invented. The
value is **separated** so each person can hold exactly the risk they want.

<Callout title="One line to remember">
  Deposit USDC, get a **fixed-rate bond (PT)** and a **yield token (YT)**. Hold PT for a
  known return, hold YT for leveraged yield, or trade either on the market.
</Callout>

## Built to the Pendle V2 architecture

Spield follows the design of Pendle V2, adapted to Stellar and its Soroban smart contracts.
Each piece has a direct counterpart:

| Spield | Role | Pendle V2 counterpart |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **SR** (Standardized Return) | A yield-bearing share token over the yield source. Everything above it speaks only SR. | SY (EIP-5115) |
| **PT** | The fixed-rate bond. A classic Stellar asset. | PT |
| **YT** | The yield claim. The yield contract itself is the YT token. | YT |
| **PT/SR market** | The AMM where PT trades against SR, with a fee that scales with time to maturity. | PendleMarket |

Each maturity is its own **series**: a separate set of six contracts with an expiry that is
set once and never changes. See [Series and maturities](/docs/how-it-works/series-and-maturities)
and [Standardized Return (SR)](/docs/how-it-works/standardized-return).

## Live on mainnet, with two yield sources

Spield has been live on Stellar mainnet since September 2026. Three mainnet series are open,
launched on 27 November 2026: **Blend USDC Series 3** (30 days), **USTRY Series 1** (30 days) and
**USTRY Series 2** (60 days). The earlier Blend Series 1 and 2 have matured and are redeem-only.

| Yield source | What it is | Where it runs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Blend USDC** | Interest paid by borrowers on Blend, Stellar's main lending market. | Mainnet and testnet |
| **Etherfuse USTRY** | Tokenized US Treasuries, priced by a NAV feed. | Mainnet (since 27 Nov 2026) and testnet |

See [The yield source](/docs/how-it-works/yield-source) and [Treasury series (USTRY)](/docs/how-it-works/treasury-series).

## The problem Spield solves

Almost all on-chain yield is **variable**. You supply USDC to a lending market and
earn whatever the rate happens to be, day to day. That is fine for some people, but it
makes two very normal things impossible:

1. **Locking in a known return.** A saver who wants "5% for 90 days, fixed" cannot
   get it, because the rate floats.
2. **Expressing a view on yield itself.** A trader who thinks rates will *rise* has no
   clean instrument to express that.

Traditional finance solved this long ago with **bonds** (fixed return) and
**interest-rate products** (a view on rates). Spield brings the same two primitives
on-chain, in a form that is transparent and verifiable.

## How Spield does it

Spield does not *create* yield. It **sources** real yield and then **restructures** it.

<div className="fd-steps">

### Real yield in

Your USDC is supplied to **Blend**. The position earns interest that accrues **on-chain**,
and the SR token tracks its value. (More in [The yield source](/docs/how-it-works/yield-source).)

### Split into PT and YT

The protocol "strips" that position into a fixed leg (PT) and a variable leg (YT). This
is called **yield stripping**, and it is the model behind the largest fixed-income
protocols in DeFi.

### Choose your exposure

Hold **PT** for a fixed return, hold **YT** for leveraged yield, provide **liquidity**
to earn fees, or **trade** PT and YT on the market as your view changes.

</div>

## Who Spield is for

| You are... | Spield gives you... |
| --- | --- |
| A **saver** who wants predictability | A fixed return locked in at deposit (PT, or the Fixed-Rate Vault). |
| A **yield trader** with a view on rates | Cheap, leveraged exposure to yield (YT). |
| A **liquidity provider** | Swap fees, plus the yield on the SR half of the pool, with little impermanent loss if you hold to maturity. |
| A **builder** | Open contracts, a typed [SDK](/docs/developers/sdk), and composable tokens to build fixed-income products on. |

</Callout>
