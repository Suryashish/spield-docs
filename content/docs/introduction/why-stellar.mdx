A yield-stripping protocol is only as good as the yield it can strip. Spield is built on
**Stellar** because Stellar offers something rare: **real, high-quality yield** plus the
performance characteristics that make a fixed-income product practical.

## Real yield to strip

Stellar has become a leading home for **regulated, real-world-asset (RWA) yield** and a
healthy native lending market. That matters because Spield's whole job is to *restructure*
yield, so the quality and realness of the underlying yield is the foundation everything
sits on. Today Spield strips two kinds:

- **Blend USDC.** Blend is Stellar's primary lending market. USDC supplied there earns genuine
  borrower-paid interest that accrues on-chain. There is no synthetic or "promised" rate.
  See [The yield source](/docs/how-it-works/yield-source).
- **Etherfuse USTRY.** Tokenized US Treasuries, priced by RedStone's NAV feed on mainnet,
  where two USTRY series have been live since 27 November 2026, and on testnet. See [Treasury series (USTRY)](/docs/how-it-works/treasury-series).

## The right performance profile

Fixed-income products involve lots of small, frequent operations: deposits, claims,
redemptions, trades, liquidity moves. That only works on a chain that is:

| Property | Why it matters for Spield |
| --- | --- |
| **Low fees** | Claiming a few cents of yield or rebalancing a small position has to be economical. |
| **Fast finality** | Trades and redemptions confirm in seconds, so the experience feels instant. |
| **Native USDC** | Circle's USDC lives on Stellar directly. It is the asset you deposit *and* the asset PT redeems into, with no wrapping. |
| **Smart contracts (Soroban)** | Stellar's contract platform runs the protocol logic securely and cheaply. |
| **Classic assets and SACs** | PT is an ordinary Stellar asset, so wallets, explorers, and the Stellar DEX understand it natively. |

## No bridges in the core

Many cross-chain "yield" products inherit risk from bridges and relayers, the parts that
have historically been the most exploited in crypto. Spield's **core protocol is
Stellar-native only**:

- The asset you deposit (USDC) is native to Stellar.
- The asset PT redeems into is the same native USDC.
- There is no cross-chain message-passing or single-signer relayer anywhere in the value path.

<Callout type="info" title="Moving USDC in from other chains is optional, and only at the edge">
  The app can move USDC from other chains to Stellar and back with **Circle CCTP**, so you
  can start from a wallet on Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum, OP Mainnet, Polygon, Avalanche, or Solana.
  That only moves funds **to your own Stellar wallet** (or out of it). Once your USDC is on
  Stellar, everything Spield does is native. Cross-chain is a convenience for getting in and
  out, never part of how the protocol holds or grows your money. See
  [Cross-chain bridge](/docs/features/cross-chain-bridge).
</Callout>

## In short

Stellar gives Spield **real yield to work with**, **fees low enough** for fixed-income
mechanics to make sense, **fast settlement**, and a **bridge-free core**. That combination
is what makes a credible on-chain bond market possible.
