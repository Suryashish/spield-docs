## The basics

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="What is Spield?">
Spield is the fixed-income layer for Stellar, built to the Pendle V2 architecture. It takes a
yield-bearing position and splits it into a fixed-rate bond (PT) and a yield token (YT), with a
market where both trade. It has been live on Stellar mainnet since September 2026.
→ [What is Spield?](/docs/introduction)
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What exactly is a PT and a YT?">
When you deposit, Spield mints equal amounts of a **Principal Token (PT)** and a **Yield Token
(YT)**. PT is a zero-coupon bond that redeems 1:1 for your principal at maturity. YT is a claim
on all the yield that position generates until maturity. Together they always equal the value
of the original position. → [PT and YT explained](/docs/how-it-works/pt-and-yt)
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What is SR?">
**SR (Standardized Return)** is a yield-bearing share token over the yield source, Spield's
counterpart to Pendle's SY. You deposit USDC and receive SR. The number of SR you hold stays flat,
and each SR is worth more USDC over time. The PT/YT engine and the market only speak SR, which is what
lets Spield add other yield sources without changing the contracts above it. The app wraps and
unwraps SR for you inside trades, and you can also hold it in the SR Wrapper.
→ [Standardized Return (SR)](/docs/how-it-works/standardized-return)
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Where does the yield actually come from?">
Today from two sources. On mainnet and testnet, **Blend**, a native Stellar lending market: your USDC
becomes a Blend supply position whose rate rises on-chain as borrowers pay interest. On testnet,
**Etherfuse USTRY**, tokenized US Treasuries, where the yield is USTRY's NAV rising. There is no
invented index and no bridged asset. → [The yield source](/docs/how-it-works/yield-source),
[Treasury series (USTRY)](/docs/how-it-works/treasury-series)
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do I need to understand PT and YT to use Spield?">
No. The **Fixed-Rate Vault** lets you deposit USDC and lock a fixed payout without ever touching PT or
YT. PT and YT are there for users who want more control. → [Fixed-Rate Vault](/docs/features/fixed-rate-vault)
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What do I deposit?">
On Blend series, **USDC**, Circle's USD-backed stablecoin, native on Stellar. On USTRY series, **USTRY**
(or USDC, which the app swaps for USTRY first). You also want a few **XLM** for network fees and
account reserves. → [Funding your wallet](/docs/getting-started/funding-your-wallet)
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Is Spield live on mainnet? Can I deposit right now?">
Yes. Three mainnet series are open, launched on 27 November 2026: **Blend USDC Series 3** (30 days,
matures 27 December 2026), **USTRY Series 1** (30 days, matures 27 December 2026) and **USTRY Series
2** (60 days, matures 26 January 2027). Their pools are small at launch and Blend Series 3 caps
deposits at 50 USDC, so check the quote before a large trade. The earlier Blend Series 1 and 2 have
**matured** and are redeem-only; you can redeem those positions at any time.
→ [Redeem at maturity](/docs/guides/redeem-at-maturity)
</Accordion>

</Accordions>

## Series and maturity

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="What is a series, and what is maturity?">
A **series** is one maturity, deployed as its own stack of six contracts (SR, strategy, yield engine,
market, vault, router) with its own PT asset. **Maturity** is the expiry date, set once when the series
is created and never changed. There is no roll: a new maturity is a new series. After maturity there are
no new mints, swaps, or liquidity additions, but PT redemption, vault receipt redemption, YT yield claims,
and liquidity removal keep working. → [Series and maturities](/docs/how-it-works/series-and-maturities)
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Why did a series stop accepting deposits?">
There are a few reasons, and none of them affect your ability to exit:

- **It matured.** Minting closes at expiry.
- **It hit its Guarded Launch Cap.** Each series has a ceiling on total SR deposits (for example 50 USDC on mainnet Blend Series 1 and 3, and 160 USDC on Series 2). It is measured against the principal deposited, and it only gates new deposits, so lowering it can never trap anyone.
- **The Fixed-Rate Vault is out of spare capacity.** The vault only promises a payout it holds PT to back, so a vault deposit reads **Not accepting deposits** or **Exceeds vault capacity** until there is room. A smaller amount may still fit.
- **An admin pause.** A pause stops new deposits and trades, while the core exits stay open.
- **On USTRY series,** new deposits and splits also close when the NAV is older than the series' maximum age or below its previous high.

If deposits are closed, you can still trade PT and YT paying in SR on a series that is below maturity
(SR trades skip the deposit cap). See [Trade with SR](/docs/guides/trade-with-sr).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Which PT asset is the real one?">
PT is a classic Stellar asset with its own issuer **per series**, and different series can use the same
asset code. Always compare the full **`CODE:ISSUER`**, never the code alone. The mainnet PT assets are:

- Series 1: `SPLDPT:GDNSUOHJIWYXX6HC6ZVDJNLSIBLZMLJYBG7NAN2CRUSXOG4FIDAOH5KN`
- Series 2: `SPLDPT:GBBRKYFRHSDUQ3YQRIFR42GZ4OKFMVKCZN7JYFPESVNSRU3WEE4JPI7Q`

A throwaway 90-minute demo series also ran on mainnet on 1 September 2026 and has matured. Its asset is
`SPLDPTD:GBNXYAM46QXDKPWKHJLONJQFJJMYOAXCHOPL7G344OIKPGSTVD4FPP7P`. Each issuer locks itself (all signer
weights set to 0), so only that series' yield contract can mint its PT. Anything else with a similar name
is not Spield's PT. Check the [Addresses](/docs/developers/addresses) page, or the app, which shows the
asset for the series you select.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can a series be extended?">
No. The expiry is set once and cannot be changed. If you want continued exposure, move to a newer series
by redeeming and depositing again.
</Accordion>

</Accordions>

## Returns and risk

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="Can I lose money holding PT?">
PT redeems 1:1 for principal at maturity, so held to maturity it returns your principal plus the locked
discount as fixed yield. Before maturity its market price moves with rates, like any bond, so selling early
could be at a gain or a loss. YT carries more risk: it can decay to zero if realized yield underperforms the
implied rate. On a USTRY series PT pays a USTRY amount, so a fall in NAV means fewer dollars.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Is the fixed rate really guaranteed?">
Once issued, the rate on your position does not change: a vault receipt, or PT you have bought, is locked.
The *quoted* rate for **new** deposits can move, but never retroactively. The guarantee is only as strong
as the backing underneath it, so it carries the protocol's risks. If the yield source lost principal, the
loss would be shared pro rata. See [Understanding the risks](/docs/resources/security).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How is the Fixed-Rate Vault's rate set?">
The vault's rate is set by the admin and is bounded on-chain by a hard ceiling of 20%. At launch, the
deployment process checks the rate against Blend's measured supply rate, net of the 5% yield fee and with a
safety margin. That check happens when the series is launched, not inside the contract. What the contract
does enforce is the ceiling and that every receipt is backed by PT the vault actually holds.
→ [Fixed-Rate Vault](/docs/features/fixed-rate-vault)
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What are the main risks?">
The honest short list: **smart-contract risk**, **yield-source risk** (Blend, or for USTRY the issuer and
the NAV feed), **YT risk** (it can decay to zero), **market risk** (PT's price moves before maturity),
**liquidity risk**, and **admin risk** (a 2-of-3 multisig controls the contracts). Full detail:
[Understanding the risks](/docs/resources/security).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Could the protocol become insolvent?">
The contracts assert solvency after every value-moving call: the engine must hold enough SR to redeem
every PT at par and pay every credited yield claim. You can read the figures yourself on the
[Solvency dashboard](/docs/features/solvency-dashboard). The residual risk is outside the contracts:
if the yield source itself lost value, that loss would flow through.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Has Spield been audited?">
Spield has not published an independent audit report, and the project describes itself as unaudited. Every
contract is verifiable on-chain, and the docs list the public addresses. Treat it as early-stage software, keep
amounts small (launch caps are small on purpose), and only deposit what you can afford to lose.
</Accordion>

</Accordions>

## Mechanics

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="Can I exit before maturity?">
Yes. Sell PT (or YT) on the market at the current price, or, if you hold equal PT and YT, **Combine** them
back into USDC at par on the Deposit page. The fixed return is what you get if you *hold to maturity*.
Exiting early means taking the current market price.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I claim my YT yield?">
Open the **Yield** page and click **Claim**. Spield pays the accrued yield in USDC (net of the yield fee) in one
signature, and your YT keeps earning until maturity. Yield accrued before maturity stays claimable forever.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What happens at maturity, and how do I redeem?">
PT redeems 1:1 for USDC, vault receipts redeem for their full fixed payout, YT stops accruing (but earned
yield stays claimable), and you can remove liquidity. Everything stays open after maturity with no deadline.
→ [Redeem at maturity](/docs/guides/redeem-at-maturity)
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do LPs suffer impermanent loss?">
The fixed-maturity, time-decay design largely limits it: as maturity approaches, PT converges to par, so an LP
who stays to maturity faces little impermanent loss while still earning swap fees. The pool pairs PT with SR,
so the SR half also keeps earning the yield source's rate. Exiting early can still realize some loss.
→ [Liquidity](/docs/features/liquidity)
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What fees does Spield charge?">
Two protocol fees, each with a hard on-chain ceiling:

- **Yield fee: 5%** of the yield that YT holders collect (ceiling 10%).
- **Swap fees** on PT/SR trades scale with time to maturity. **80% goes to liquidity providers and 20% to the treasury** (the treasury share has a ceiling of 50%).

There are also the usual tiny **Stellar network fees** in XLM, and Circle's fee on cross-chain transfers
(shown before you confirm). Holding PT to maturity or using the vault involves no trading fee; the vault's
payout is quoted net of everything. → [Protocol fees](/docs/features/protocol-fees)
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What is USTRY, and can I use it on mainnet?">
**USTRY** is Etherfuse's tokenized US Treasury product on Stellar. Spield runs a USTRY series family
(deposit USTRY, split into PT and YT, trade in a PT/USTRY pool, finalize at maturity). It is live on
**testnet today** and planned for mainnet. A USTRY series is priced by a NAV feed (SEP-40), pays in USTRY
tokens rather than dollars, and does not guarantee dollar principal. Exits and maturity never wait on the
feed. → [Treasury yield guide](/docs/guides/treasury-yield), [Treasury series](/docs/how-it-works/treasury-series)
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I bring USDC from other chains?">
Yes, with **Circle CCTP V2**. The mainnet app supports Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum, OP Mainnet, Polygon,
Avalanche, and Solana. USDC is burned on the source chain and native USDC is minted to your own Stellar
wallet. You can also pay directly from another chain on the Vault, SR Wrapper, Liquidity, Deposit, and
Markets panels, and send exits back out. Journeys are resumable and funds always stop in your own wallet if
anything is interrupted. → [Bridge USDC](/docs/guides/bridge-usdc)
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Is there an SDK?">
Yes. [`@spield/sdk`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@spield/sdk) on npm covers mainnet and testnet, Blend
and USTRY series: quotes, buying and selling PT and YT, redeeming, claiming yield, trustlines, a wallet
picker, and a typed `error.reason` for every refusal. New series appear through a signed hosted list
without an SDK release. → [SDK](/docs/developers/sdk)
</Accordion>

</Accordions>

## Trust and safety

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="Is there any bridge or cross-chain risk in the core?">
No. Spield's core protocol is **Stellar-native only**. Both the deposit and settlement asset (USDC) live on
Stellar, with no relayer in the value path. Cross-chain USDC through Circle CCTP only helps you get funds
into or out of your own wallet, and carries Circle CCTP's own risk if you use it.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Who controls the protocol? Can an admin take my funds?">
The contracts' admin is a **2-of-3 multisig** (`GDJ66WMVVH6MWBL4KORY2HOXWLY4UGFCMP64RC474LUJPQCPQUC4RUFL`). Its
powers are bounded: pausing new deposits and trades, adjusting caps, rates, and fees within on-chain ceilings,
and scheduling upgrades behind a timelock of at least 24 hours. There is no function that moves user balances
or mints unbacked tokens, and exits stay open while paused.
→ [Architecture: trust model](/docs/developers/architecture#trust-model-honest-summary)
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How can I verify the protocol is solvent right now?">
Open the [Solvency dashboard](/docs/features/solvency-dashboard) in the app, or query the figures directly
from the chain, as described in [reading state](/docs/developers/reading-state). The numbers are live and
permissionless to read.
</Accordion>
