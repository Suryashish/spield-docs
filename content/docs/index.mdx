**Spield** turns real, on-chain Stellar yield into building blocks anyone can use:
a **fixed-rate bond** you can hold to a known return, a **leveraged yield token**
for those who want upside, and a **trading venue** where both change hands.

It follows the architecture of Pendle V2 and is **live on Stellar mainnet** (since September 2026).
Deposit USDC, then choose **certainty**, **leverage**, or **fees**. The protocol does the rest,
with backing that you can check on-chain.

## New here? Start with the idea

Most yield in DeFi is **variable**. It moves with the market, and you never know
exactly what you will earn. Spield lets you **fix it**: lock a known return by a known
date, the same way a bond works in traditional finance. Read [What is Spield?](/docs/introduction)
for the two-minute version.

## Where things stand

- **App:** [app.spield.live](https://app.spield.live). Marketing and Learn live at [www.spield.live](https://www.spield.live).
- **Mainnet:** Series 1 and Series 2 (Blend USDC) have both matured. They are redeem-only. New series are announced in the app and in the SDK's hosted series list. See [Series and maturities](/docs/how-it-works/series-and-maturities).
- **Testnet:** open short series on Blend USDC, plus a tokenized-Treasury series on Etherfuse USTRY. See [Treasury series (USTRY)](/docs/how-it-works/treasury-series).
- **Developers:** [`@spield/sdk`](/docs/developers/sdk) on npm covers mainnet and testnet.
