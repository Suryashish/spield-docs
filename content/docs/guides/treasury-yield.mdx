Spield's second yield source is **Etherfuse USTRY**, a tokenized US Treasury product on Stellar. A
USTRY series works like a Blend series (deposit, split into PT and YT, trade, redeem), but the asset
you deposit is **USTRY**, not USDC, and its value comes from a **NAV** that a price feed reports.

<Callout type="warn" title="Real funds on mainnet, and not a dollar guarantee">
  USTRY series are live on **Stellar mainnet** since 27 November 2026 (USTRY Series 1, 30 days, and
  USTRY Series 2, 60 days), with small pools and caps at launch. A USTRY series pays out in **USTRY
  tokens**. PT is measured in US dollar
  *face*, but it is redeemed for a USTRY amount fixed at the series' high-water NAV, so if NAV
  falls, that USTRY is worth fewer dollars. USD face is **not** a principal guarantee.
  See [Understanding the risks](/docs/resources/security).
</Callout>

For the design behind this, see [Treasury series (USTRY)](/docs/how-it-works/treasury-series).

## How a USTRY series differs from a Blend series

| | Blend series | USTRY series |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Deposit asset | USDC | USTRY (or USDC, swapped for USTRY on the Stellar DEX first) |
| Yield | Blend's lending rate, through SR | USTRY's NAV rising |
| PT, YT | PT is a classic asset (trustline); YT is a contract token | Both are **contract claim tokens**, so no PT trustline |
| Receipts | SR | **sUSTRY**, an unsplit receipt: one sUSTRY holds one USTRY |
| Price source | None (read from Blend) | A **SEP-40** NAV feed (a Spield-run feed on testnet, RedStone on mainnet) |
| Market | PT/SR pool | PT/USTRY pool |

Three rules explain most of what you will see in the app:

- **Only new deposits and splits read the NAV feed.** If the NAV is older than the series' maximum age
  (25 hours on the mainnet series, 13 hours on testnet), or below its previous high, new deposits and splits close. Exits, claims,
  YT transfers, and burns **never call the feed**, so a stale feed cannot trap you.
- **The high-water NAV never falls.** It can rise by at most a capped amount per year (8% a year on
  the mainnet series, 20% on testnet), so a bad price cannot shift value from PT to YT faster than that.
- **NAV is never read from DEX trades.**

## Where to run it

The hosted app at [app.spield.live](https://app.spield.live) runs on **mainnet**. Pick **USTRY** in
the header's family menu, then a series:

| Series | Term | Matures (UTC) | SDK id |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **USTRY Series 2** | 60 days | 2027-01-26 17:51 | `mainnet-ustry-2` |
| **USTRY Series 1** | 30 days | 2026-12-27 17:49 | `mainnet-ustry-1` |

The steps below follow those screens. The [SDK](/docs/developers/sdk) does every step in code. To
practise without real funds, use **testnet** (a testnet build of the app, or the SDK on testnet),
where the same contracts run on sandbox USTRY; there, do not use the old testnet **USTRY Series 1**,
which ran on a retired design.

## Step by step

<Steps>

<Step>
### Get XLM and trustlines

Fund your wallet with XLM for the base reserve and fees (see
[Funding your wallet](/docs/getting-started/funding-your-wallet)), then add the trustline for
USTRY, and for USDC if you want to pay in USDC:

- **Mainnet:** Etherfuse USTRY `USTRY:GCRYUGD5NVARGXT56XEZI5CIFCQETYHAPQQTHO2O3IQZTHDH4LATMYWC`
  and Circle USDC `USDC:GA5ZSEJYB37JRC5AVCIA5MOP4RHTM335X2KGX3IHOJAPP5RE34K4KZVN`.
- **Testnet:** fund with Friendbot; sandbox USTRY
  `USTRY:GC3CW7EDYRTWQ635VDIGY6S4ZUF5L6TQ7AA4MWS7LEQDBLUSZXV7UPS4` and Circle test USDC.
</Step>

<Step>
### Get USTRY

Two options:

- **In the app:** open the **SR Wrapper** page for the USTRY series. Under **Pay with** pick USDC and under **Receive in** pick USTRY. The button reads **Buy USTRY with USDC**. It is a plain swap on the Stellar DEX, and no sUSTRY is minted.
- **On the DEX directly:** on mainnet, swap Circle USDC for USTRY; the order book trades within a few
  basis points of NAV. On testnet, swap XLM for sandbox USTRY (100 XLM bought about 60 USTRY when the
  SDK documentation was written).

The launch pools are small on both networks: on mainnet USTRY Series 2 takes a single PT buy of
about $80 and Series 1 about $3. Read the quote before you trade.
</Step>

<Step>
### Deposit and mint PT and YT

On the **Deposit** page, choose how you pay and enter an amount:

- **Pay with USTRY:** one signature. The series splits it into equal PT and YT in USD face at the current NAV.
- **Pay with USDC:** the app first swaps USDC for USTRY on the Stellar DEX, then mints. That adds a signature. The **How your funds move** strip shows each step.

The button reads **Deposit & Mint**. If it is disabled, the page says why: NAV is stale or below its
previous high, the series is paused or full, or it has matured.
</Step>

<Step>
### Or deposit as sUSTRY, and split later

Prefer to hold an unsplit receipt? On **SR Wrapper**, **Wrap** USTRY into **sUSTRY**. A receipt needs no
price, and you can unwrap it one for one at any time, even if the feed is down. When you want PT and YT,
use **Split receipts into PT + YT** (one signature, no swap). Splitting needs a fresh NAV, like a deposit.
</Step>

<Step>
### Trade PT and YT

If the series has a PT/USTRY pool, the **Markets** page trades PT and YT, paying with **USTRY** (one signature), **USDC** (a DEX swap first, so one more signature), or **sUSTRY** (a receipt withdrawal first, one more signature). Selling works the same way in reverse.

- Buying **PT** locks a fixed rate in NAV growth to maturity.
- Buying **YT** earns the USTRY freed whenever NAV rises above its previous high. YT can only be bought while NAV is fresh and not in a drawdown. PT trades and YT sales work regardless.
- **Liquidity** pairs PT with USTRY and earns swap fees, PT's pull to par, and NAV growth on the USTRY half. Withdrawals stay open while paused, after maturity, and during an oracle outage.
</Step>

<Step>
### Claim yield

On the **Yield** page you see **Claimable now** in USTRY, your **YT face held**, and the **High-water NAV**.
Click to claim. Your YT is untouched. Claiming reads no feed, but it may first fold the latest published
NAV into the series (a sync) so the claim includes it. A recovery below the previous high earns nothing new.
</Step>

<Step>
### Maturity: finalize

When the series matures, the series stays open until someone **finalizes** it. This freezes PT's
and YT's USTRY allocation once. The **Awaiting finalization** card appears on the Deposit and Yield pages
with a **Finalize series** button, and **anyone** can press it. It runs when a NAV sample from after
maturity exists, or once the **grace period** (7 days on the mainnet series, 3 days on testnet)
has passed. After the grace
period it does not read the feed at all, so finalization cannot be blocked. It may take two signatures:
the first reads the latest NAV into the series so no yield is dropped, the second finalizes.
</Step>

<Step>
### Redeem

After finalization, a **PT holder redeems PT alone** for USTRY at the frozen allocation. You do not need YT.
Unclaimed YT yield stays claimable with no deadline. Before maturity, equal PT and YT can be combined
back into USTRY. Unsplit sUSTRY can be withdrawn at any time, with no finalization. You can also
sell the USTRY for USDC on the way out, which depends on DEX liquidity. More in
[Redeem at maturity](/docs/guides/redeem-at-maturity).
</Step>

</Steps>

## The same flow in code

The [SDK](/docs/developers/sdk) wraps this in a few calls (amounts are in the series asset, USTRY):

```ts
import { SpieldClient } from '@spield/sdk';

const spield = new SpieldClient({ signer });        // testnet by default
await spield.loadSeries();
const series = spield.ustrySeries('testnet-ustry-2');

await series.ensureTrustlines();                    // USTRY trustline first
const info = await series.getInfo();                // implied rate, liquidity, warnings
await series.buyPt({ amount: '1' });                // lock a fixed rate with 1 USTRY
// ... after maturity:
await series.claimYield();
await series.redeem();                              // runs finalize first if needed
```

`redeem()` adds the `finalize` step for you when the series has not been finalized yet.

<Callout type="info" title="Why USTRY Series 1 was replaced">
  The first USTRY test series needed a fresh price for almost every action, so a late price could block
  exits. Series 2 was redesigned so exits and maturity never wait on the feed. That is why only new
  deposits and splits depend on a fresh NAV.
</Callout>
