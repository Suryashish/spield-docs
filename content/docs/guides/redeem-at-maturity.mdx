Every Spield series has a fixed **maturity**. When it passes, the series stops taking new money and
settles. This guide covers everything you can collect afterwards. It applies today to the two
**matured mainnet series** (Series 1 and Series 2) and to any testnet series that has ended.

## What stops and what keeps working

| After maturity | Status |
| --- | --- |
| New deposits and mints | **Closed** |
| Swaps on the PT/SR market | **Closed** |
| Adding liquidity | **Closed** |
| Redeeming PT for USDC, 1:1 | **Open** |
| Redeeming Fixed-Rate Vault receipts | **Open** |
| Claiming YT yield | **Open**, no deadline |
| Removing liquidity | **Open** |
| Unwrapping SR to USDC | **Open** (it has no maturity) |

Nothing is force-closed, and nothing expires out from under you. Maturity is set once when a series
is created and can never be moved.

## Find your matured series

In the app header, open the **Series / maturity** menu. Ended series are tagged **Ended**, for example
`Series 2 · Oct 26, 2026 · Ended`. Choose the series you deposited into. The Deposit page then
switches to **Redeem** on its own, and the other pages show closed controls.

The mainnet series, for reference:

| Series | Matured (UTC) | PT asset |

## Redeem PT (1:1)

PT redeems for exactly its face value in USDC. You do not need YT after maturity.

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open Deposit

Select the matured series, then **Deposit** in the sidebar. The page is already in **Redeem** mode (the
Deposit tab reads **Deposit (Closed)**).
</Step>

<Step>
### Enter the PT amount

The field reads **You burn** with PT. **You receive (USDC)** shows the same number: par, with no curve and
no slippage. The **Receive on** menu can send the USDC to another chain instead of your Stellar wallet.
</Step>

<Step>
### Redeem

Click **Redeem at par**. It is one signature: the router burns your PT and withdraws the USDC
from Blend in the same call.
</Step>

</Steps>

Before maturity the same button reads **Combine** and needs equal PT and YT. After maturity, PT alone is enough.

## Redeem a Fixed-Rate Vault receipt

1. Open **Fixed Vault** for the matured series.
2. Under **Your fixed-rate receipts**, each matured receipt shows **Matured** and a **Redeem** button.
3. Click **Redeem**. You are paid the receipt's full fixed payout (principal plus coupon) in USDC. The
   **Receive on** menu beside it can send the USDC to another chain.

If Blend cannot pay the whole payout at once, the vault pays what it can and the receipt shows
**Partly withdrawn** with a **Collect rest** button. Press it again later for the remainder.

## Claim YT yield

All yield accrued **before** maturity stays claimable forever. After maturity the yield index
is frozen at the expiry time and no more accrues.

1. Open **Yield** (under **Earn**) for the series.
2. **Claimable now** shows what you are owed, net of the protocol yield fee.
3. Click **Claim ... USDC**. One signature, paid in USDC.

A matured YT has no principal claim, so it is not needed to redeem PT.

## Remove liquidity

1. Open **Liquidity** and switch to **Remove**.
2. Choose how much with the slider and click **Remove Liquidity**. You get your share of the pool back as **PT and SR**.
3. Redeem the PT at par on the Deposit page (above), and unwrap the SR in the **SR Wrapper** with
   **Unwrap to USDC**. The **Receive on** menu can do both and send USDC to another chain.

## Unwrap SR

If you hold SR, open the **SR Wrapper** and use **Unwrap to USDC**. It has no maturity gate, so it works
at any time. If Blend cannot pay everything at once the button reads **Withdraw what is available**.

## A USTRY series

USTRY series add one step. A matured series must be **finalized** once before PT can redeem, and anyone can
do it from the **Awaiting finalization** card. Unsplit **sUSTRY** receipts can be withdrawn any time without
finalizing. See [Treasury yield (USTRY on testnet)](/docs/guides/treasury-yield).

## Redeem everything with the SDK

The [SDK](/docs/developers/sdk) can settle all matured positions for a wallet in one call:

```ts
import { SpieldClient, mainnet } from '@spield/sdk';

const spield = new SpieldClient({ network: mainnet(), signer });
await spield.loadSeries();
await spield.redeemMatured();   // redeem PT, claim yield, redeem vault receipts
```

Use `preview('redeem')` on a series first to simulate without signing. Mainnet moves real USDC and
costs real XLM in fees.

<Callout type="warn" title="Redemption is par, not a guarantee against loss">
  PT redeems 1:1 in USDC terms because SR is always redeemable for the underlying. If Blend were to lose
  principal, SR holders, PT redeemers, and vault receipts share the loss pro rata. See
  [Understanding the risks](/docs/resources/security).
</Callout>
