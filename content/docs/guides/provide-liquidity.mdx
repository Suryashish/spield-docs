Providing liquidity earns you a share of every swap fee: **80% of each fee goes to liquidity
providers and 20% to the protocol treasury**. The pool pairs **PT with SR**, so the SR half of your
position keeps earning the yield source's rate while it sits in the pool. LPs who hold to maturity
also face little impermanent loss. See [Liquidity](/docs/features/liquidity) for why.

## Before you start

You need **PT** and a way to pay the other side. You supply PT from your wallet, and the app turns USDC
into the matching SR for you.

- If you do not hold PT in this series yet, get some first: [mint PT + YT](/docs/features/pt-yt-tokens) on the Deposit page, or buy PT on Markets.
- You need a PT trustline for the series (the app shows **Enable PT**).
- The series must be open. At maturity the pool is closed to new liquidity, and you can only withdraw.

## Add liquidity

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open the Liquidity page

Select **Liquidity** in the app sidebar and make sure **Add** is selected.
</Step>

<Step>
### Enter your PT amount

Type the **PT amount** you want to supply. The app **mirrors** the matching amount at the pool's
current ratio in **USDC required**, so you add balanced liquidity without moving the price. The
**Pay with** menu next to USDC lets you pay from another chain instead (see [Bridge USDC](/docs/guides/bridge-usdc)).
</Step>

<Step>
### Confirm

Click **Add Liquidity** (or **Enable PT** first). Adding from your Stellar wallet is **two
signatures**, shown in the **How your funds move** strip: USDC is supplied to Blend for SR, then
the SR is added to the pool together with your PT. You receive **LP shares**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Earn fees

From the next trade onward your shares accrue a portion of the swap fees. Your claim on the pool
grows over time, and the SR half keeps earning yield.
</Step>

</Steps>

## Monitor your position

The **Your liquidity** panel shows your **LP shares** and the PT and USDC they currently redeem
for at the live price. Because the pool's composition shifts as people trade, your PT and USDC split
will move around. That is normal.

## Remove liquidity

<Steps>

<Step>
### Choose how much to withdraw

Switch to **Remove** and use the slider under **Amount to remove** to take part or all of your
position.
</Step>

<Step>
### Confirm

Click **Remove Liquidity** and approve in your wallet. One signature returns your share of the
pool as **PT and SR**, including accrued fees.
</Step>

<Step>
### Turn it back into USDC (optional)

You can leave the SR to keep earning, unwrap it in the **SR Wrapper**, or sell the PT on **Markets**.
Before maturity, the **Receive on** menu can also send USDC straight to another chain (the app
sells the PT side and unwraps the SR as part of the journey). After maturity the PT redeems at par.
See [Redeem at maturity](/docs/guides/redeem-at-maturity).
</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout title="You can always exit">
  Removing liquidity is never blocked. It stays open after maturity and during an emergency
  pause. There is no lock-up.
</Callout>

## Tips

- **Hold to maturity for the best result.** That is when the low-impermanent-loss property is strongest, because PT's drift to par is fully priced in.
- **Returns track volume.** More trading through the pool means more fees for LPs.
- **Add at the current ratio.** The app does this for you. It keeps things fair and avoids price impact.

<Callout type="warn" title="Impermanent loss is not fully zero">
  "Little" applies to PT's predictable march to par when you hold to maturity. Unexpected
  yield swings can still cause some divergence if you exit early. See
  [risks](/docs/resources/security).
</Callout>

Need USDC from another chain first? See [Bridge USDC to Stellar](/docs/guides/bridge-usdc).
