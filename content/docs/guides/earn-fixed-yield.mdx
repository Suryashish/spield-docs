Locking a fixed return is Spield's core promise. There are two ways to do it: the **easy
way** (the Fixed-Rate Vault) and the **hands-on way** (buy PT directly). Both lock a known
return. Pick based on how much control you want.

Both start the same way: connect a wallet, then choose an **open series** in the header's
**Series / maturity** menu. (A series that shows **Ended** only supports redemption. See
[Redeem at maturity](/docs/guides/redeem-at-maturity).)

<Tabs items={['Easy: Fixed-Rate Vault', 'Hands-on: buy PT']}>

<Tab value="Easy: Fixed-Rate Vault">

The vault hides all the machinery. Deposit USDC, get a receipt for a fixed payout.

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open the Fixed Vault

In the app sidebar, select **Fixed Vault**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Enter your amount

Type the USDC you want to deposit. The **Fixed payout at maturity** box shows your total
payout and the **fixed coupon** on top of principal. Below it you see the **Locked APR** and the
**Matures** date.
</Step>

<Step>
### Lock the rate

Click **Lock Fixed Rate** and approve in your wallet. It is one signature, and the vault needs
no PT trustline, because the vault holds the PT itself.
</Step>

<Step>
### Redeem at maturity

After the maturity date, return to the Fixed Vault page and click **Redeem** on your receipt
under **Your fixed-rate receipts**. You can also pick **Receive on** another chain to be paid out
there. If the pool cannot pay everything in one go, the button reads **Collect rest**, and
you can collect the remainder later.
</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout title="Your return is locked">
  From the moment you deposit, your payout is fixed. Nothing the market does afterward changes
  it. The payout is backed by PT the vault holds, and the vault asserts that backing after every
  deposit.
</Callout>

If the button reads **Not accepting deposits** or **Exceeds vault capacity**, the vault's spare
backing (or the series cap) cannot cover a coupon of that size. Try a smaller amount, or pick
another series. See the [FAQ](/docs/faq).

</Tab>

<Tab value="Hands-on: buy PT">

Buying PT yourself lets you hold the bond directly, and sell it later if you want.

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open Markets and choose PT, then Buy

Select **Markets**. Under **Asset** choose **PT**, and under **Direction** choose **Buy PT**
(its sub-label reads "Lock a fixed return").
</Step>

<Step>
### Check the implied rate

The market's **implied APY** is the fixed return you would lock by buying PT now and holding to
maturity. If you like it, continue.
</Step>

<Step>
### Pick how you pay, and enter an amount

The **Pay with** menu lets you pay in **USDC** (default), in **SR** if you already hold it, or in
USDC from another chain. Enter the amount. The app shows how much PT you will receive. You can set
a **Max slippage** (0.1%, 0.5%, 1%, 3%, or 5%, with 1% as the default).

Paying with USDC is **one signature**: the app wraps USDC into SR and buys PT in the same
transaction. Paying with SR is also one signature and skips the Blend step. See
[Trade with SR](/docs/guides/trade-with-sr).
</Step>

<Step>
### Enable PT, then buy

The first time you buy PT in a series the button reads **Add the PT trustline first**. Click it
once, approve, and then click **Buy PT with USDC**. You now hold PT.
</Step>

<Step>
### Hold, then redeem

Hold PT to maturity, then redeem it **1:1 for USDC**. On the **Deposit** page, switch to
**Redeem** and use **Redeem at par**. Full steps are in [Redeem at maturity](/docs/guides/redeem-at-maturity).
</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout type="info" title="Exiting early">
  Want out before maturity? Choose **Sell PT** on Markets and take the current price, or if you
  also hold matching YT, use **Combine** on the Deposit page to turn equal PT and YT back into USDC
  at par. Either way you take the *current* price, not the locked one.
</Callout>

</Tab>

</Tabs>

## How your return is calculated

Your fixed return comes from the **discount**: you acquire principal worth 1.00 for less than
1.00 today, and it is worth exactly 1.00 at maturity.

> Example: buy PT at **0.97** with **90 days** to maturity. At maturity it is worth **1.00**.
> That is about **3.1% over 90 days** (roughly 12.5% annualized), locked in now.

The Fixed-Rate Vault quotes the same idea as a payout: principal plus a coupon at the
vault's rate for the time left.

## What can go wrong?

- **You sell early at a loss.** Before maturity, PT's price moves with rates. If you sell early when rates have risen, you might get less than you paid. Held to maturity, you get par.
- **Yield-source risk.** Spield's backing depends on the underlying lending market. If that market lost principal, redemptions share the loss. See [risks](/docs/resources/security).
- **A series that is full or matured.** Each series has a launch cap and a fixed expiry. See the [FAQ](/docs/faq).

That is it: the simplest, lowest-risk way to use Spield. Next, the opposite bet:
[go long on yield](/docs/guides/long-yield).
