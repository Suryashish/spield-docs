The PT/SR market trades PT and YT against **SR**, the yield-bearing share token. You do not have to
think in SR: the app can take USDC and do the wrapping for you. But if you already hold SR, or want
to end up holding it, you can trade straight in SR and skip the Blend step entirely.

On the **Markets** page, two menus decide the route:

- **Pay with** (when you buy): **USDC**, **SR**, or **USDC on another chain**.
- **Receive in** (when you sell): **USDC**, **SR**, or **USDC on another chain**.

Under the form, a **How your funds move** strip shows each hop (USDC, Blend pool, SR, PT/SR market)
and how many wallet signatures the route needs.

## What SR is, in one minute

SR is a **share** of the yield source. The number you hold stays flat, and each SR is worth more USDC
over time. The app always shows an SR amount alongside its USDC worth at the current rate. Read
[Standardized Return (SR)](/docs/how-it-works/standardized-return) for the full picture.

## Signatures per route

Trading against the pool is always done in SR. Going through USDC adds a Blend hop (a supply or
a withdrawal), and a Blend hop plus a curve trade only fits in one Soroban transaction for PT.

| You do | Route | Signatures |
| --- | --- | :---: |
| Buy PT with **USDC** | USDC to SR to PT, through the router | **1** |
| Buy PT with **SR** | SR straight to the market | **1** |
| Buy YT with **USDC** | Wrap USDC into SR, then buy YT | **2** (1 if you already hold enough SR) |
| Buy YT with **SR** | SR straight to the market | **1** |
| Sell PT for **USDC** | PT to SR to USDC, through the router | **1** |
| Sell PT for **SR** | Market pays SR, no Blend withdrawal | **1** |
| Sell YT for **USDC** | Sell YT for SR, then unwrap | **2** |
| Sell YT for **SR** | Market pays SR, no Blend withdrawal | **1** |
| Pay with or receive **USDC on another chain** | Circle CCTP, then the Stellar route above | Source chain plus the Stellar signatures |

The **button text** tells you where you are on a two-signature route: **Signature 1 of 2**, then
**2 of 2**.

<Callout type="info" title="Why paying in SR is lighter">
  Paying with SR skips the Blend supply, so a buy never touches the series' deposit cap. That
  matters when a series is near or at its cap: USDC deposits are refused, but an SR holder can
  still trade.
</Callout>

## Buy PT or YT

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open Markets and choose what you want

Under **Asset** pick **PT** (a fixed return) or **YT** (leveraged yield), and under **Direction** pick
**Buy**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Choose how you pay

In **Pay with**, pick **USDC** or **SR**. SR shows your balance. The field reads **You pay (USDC)**, or
**You spend (USDC max budget)** for YT, where you give a budget and the app works out the size.
</Step>

<Step>
### Review the quote

You see what you receive, the **fixed return** for PT or the **leverage** for YT, the route strip, and a
**Max slippage** choice (0.1%, 0.5%, 1%, 3%, or 5%, with 1% as the default). Paying SR shows its USDC
worth, too.
</Step>

<Step>
### Sign

Approve in your wallet. For a PT purchase you first need the PT trustline (the button reads
**Add the PT trustline first**). A YT purchase needs none.
</Step>

</Steps>

## Sell PT or YT

Choose **Sell**, then pick **Receive in**:

- **USDC**: the app sells for SR and unwraps through Blend. PT does it in one signature, YT in two.
- **SR**: one signature, straight from the market. You keep earning the Blend rate on the SR, and can unwrap it later in the **SR Wrapper**.
- **USDC on another chain**: the sale and unwrap happen on Stellar, then Circle carries the USDC across and you sign once on the destination chain. See [Bridge USDC](/docs/guides/bridge-usdc).

<Callout type="info" title="Selling YT keeps your earned yield">
  Selling YT credits the yield you have already earned without paying it out, so it stays claimable on
  the **Yield** page even after your YT balance reaches zero.
</Callout>

## Holding SR on purpose: the SR Wrapper

The **SR Wrapper** page (under **Trade**) lets you hold SR itself, a yield-bearing dollar with no
maturity. **Wrap into SR** takes USDC in one signature. **Unwrap to USDC** takes it back, with no
maturity gate and even when deposits are paused. If Blend cannot pay everything at once, the button
reads **Withdraw what is available**, and you can unwrap the rest later.

You never need SR to trade PT or YT. It is a convenience for people who want to hold the share
directly, skip Blend hops, or provide liquidity.

## Limits to know

- **Matured series have no market.** After maturity the Markets page reads "The market is closed" and points you to **Redeem at par** on the Deposit page. See [Redeem at maturity](/docs/guides/redeem-at-maturity).
- **Big trades move the price.** The pool curve charges a large fill at the rate it leaves behind. When splitting would clearly help, the app says so and suggests a number of smaller trades.
- **Thin pools.** If the pool cannot fill your size, the app says so rather than sending a bad trade. Try a smaller amount.
