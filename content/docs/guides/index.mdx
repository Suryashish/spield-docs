## Choosing a guide

| Your goal | Guide | Risk |
| --- | --- | :---: |
| A predictable, fixed return | [Earn a fixed yield](/docs/guides/earn-fixed-yield) | Low |
| Bet that yields will rise | [Go long on yield](/docs/guides/long-yield) | Higher |
| Earn income from trading activity | [Provide liquidity](/docs/guides/provide-liquidity) | Medium |
| Pay or receive in SR, or skip a Blend hop | [Trade with SR](/docs/guides/trade-with-sr) | Same as the trade |
| Earn on tokenized US Treasuries (testnet) | [Treasury yield](/docs/guides/treasury-yield) | Higher |
| Move USDC between chains and Stellar | [Bridge USDC](/docs/guides/bridge-usdc) | Bridge risk |
| Collect from a series that has ended | [Redeem at maturity](/docs/guides/redeem-at-maturity) | Low |
