Before your Stellar wallet can hold a classic asset, it needs a **trustline** to that asset.
This is a normal, one-time part of using any Stellar token, and for Spield's PT the app
handles it with a single click. Here is exactly what needs one and what does not.

## What is a trustline?

On Stellar, an account does not automatically hold every asset in existence. It
explicitly opts in to the assets it is willing to hold by creating a **trustline** to each one.
Think of it as ticking a box that says *"yes, I am willing to receive and hold this token."*

- Without a trustline to an asset, your wallet **cannot receive** that asset. A transfer would
  simply fail.
- With a trustline, the asset shows up in your wallet and you can hold, send, and use it
  normally.

<Callout title="Why Stellar works this way">
  Requiring an explicit opt-in keeps wallets tidy and protects you from being spammed with
  unwanted tokens. It is a deliberate Stellar design choice, not a Spield-specific step.
</Callout>

## Which Spield assets need a trustline?

| Asset | Kind | Trustline? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **USDC** (Circle) | Classic asset | **Yes.** You usually already have it from when you first received USDC. |
| **PT** | Classic asset, one **per series** | **Yes, a new one for each series.** |
| **YT** | Contract token. The yield contract itself is the YT token. | **No.** It is not a classic asset, so there is nothing to trust. |
| **SR** | Contract token | **No.** |
| **Fixed-Rate Vault receipts** | Records held by the vault contract | **No.** The vault holds the PT itself. |
| **LP shares** | Tracked by the market contract | **No.** |
| **USTRY** (testnet) | Classic asset issued by Etherfuse | **Yes**, to hold or trade USTRY. |
| **sUSTRY, USTRY PT and YT** | Contract (SEP-41) claim tokens | **No.** |

### Why PT needs a new trustline for every series

PT is a classic Stellar asset (code `SPLDPT` on mainnet), wrapped as a contract token (SAC) for
the Soroban contracts. **Each series gets its own PT issuer account.** That issuer locks itself
the moment it is set up (all signer weights are set to 0), so nobody can mint PT except the
series' yield contract.

The result is that PT from different series are different assets, even when the code is the
same. On mainnet, Series 1 PT is `SPLDPT:GDNSUOHJIWYXX6HC6ZVDJNLSIBLZMLJYBG7NAN2CRUSXOG4FIDAOH5KN` and
Series 2 PT is `SPLDPT:GBBRKYFRHSDUQ3YQRIFR42GZ4OKFMVKCZN7JYFPESVNSRU3WEE4JPI7Q`. A trustline to
one does nothing for the other. Always compare the full **`CODE:ISSUER`**. The authoritative list is on the
[Addresses](/docs/developers/addresses) page, and the app shows the right asset for the series you
have selected.

## You do not set these up manually

You **do not** need to add the PT trustline by hand. The first time you do something that
delivers PT to your wallet for a series (a **Deposit**, a **Markets** buy of PT, or
**Liquidity**), the app shows an **Enable PT** button (on Markets it reads **Add the PT
trustline first**). Clicking it creates the trustline in one approval.

<Steps>

<Step>
### Start the action

On [Deposit](/docs/features/pt-yt-tokens), Markets, or Liquidity, pick your series and enter an
amount. If your wallet does not yet trust that series' PT, the app detects it and shows
the one-time setup.
</Step>

<Step>
### Click "Enable PT"

The button creates a trustline to that series' PT asset. It takes one signature. No USDC moves.
</Step>

<Step>
### Approve in your wallet

Your wallet pops up to approve adding the trustline. This is a standard Stellar operation, not a
transfer of funds.
</Step>

<Step>
### Continue

With the trustline in place, your deposit or trade proceeds and PT lands in your wallet. You are
not asked again for that series.
</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout type="info" title="Which actions do not need one">
  Buying **YT** delivers no PT, so it needs no PT trustline. The **Fixed-Rate Vault** and the
  **SR Wrapper** never need one either: the vault keeps the PT, and SR is a contract token.
</Callout>

## What does a trustline cost?

A trustline reserves **0.5 XLM** (a standard Stellar account reserve). It stays locked while the
trustline exists and is returned if you remove it. It is not a fee paid to Spield. This is why you
keep a few XLM in your wallet (see [funding your wallet](/docs/getting-started/funding-your-wallet)).
With a new PT trustline for every series, remove old ones once a series is fully redeemed if you
want the reserve back.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Likely cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "Enable PT" keeps appearing | The trustline transaction was not approved or confirmed | Click again and approve in your wallet. Make sure you have a little XLM for the reserve. |
| Deposit button says "Enable PT" after switching series | A new series has a new PT issuer | Enable PT again for the new series. |
| A PT balance does not show in the app | The wallet trusts a PT of a different series (same code, different issuer) | Compare the full `CODE:ISSUER` against [Addresses](/docs/developers/addresses). |
| Not enough XLM | No XLM for the reserve or fee | Add a few XLM (see [funding](/docs/getting-started/funding-your-wallet)). |

Once your trustline is set, you are ready for [your first deposit](/docs/getting-started/your-first-deposit).
