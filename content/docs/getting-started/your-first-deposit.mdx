You are connected and funded, so it is time to deposit. The app is at
[app.spield.live](https://app.spield.live). The **simplest** first experience is the
**Fixed-Rate Vault**: deposit USDC, get a receipt for a fixed payout at maturity. You
never touch PT or YT directly.

## Step 0: pick a series

Every deposit goes into one **series**, which is one maturity with its own contracts and its own cap.

In the app header there are two menus:

- **Yield source**: **Blend** (USDC lending) or **USTRY** (tokenized Treasuries). Only sources available on the network the app is on are listed.
- **Series / maturity**: each entry reads like `Series 2 · Oct 26, 2026`, and ended series carry an **Ended** tag.

Choose a series that has not ended. Deposits and trades only work on series that are still open.

<Callout type="warn" title="No mainnet series is open for deposits right now">
  Both mainnet series (Series 1 and Series 2) have matured. If you pick one, the Fixed Vault and Deposit
  pages show their deposit buttons as closed (for example **Vault Matured (Deposits Closed)**),
  and what remains is [redeeming at maturity](/docs/guides/redeem-at-maturity). To practice the
  whole flow, use a live **testnet** series. New mainnet series are announced in the app.
</Callout>

## The easy path: the Fixed-Rate Vault

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open the Fixed Vault

In the app sidebar, under **Earn**, choose **Fixed Vault**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Enter an amount

Type how much USDC you want to deposit. The app shows a **live quote**: your **fixed payout at
maturity** (principal plus a fixed coupon), the **Locked APR**, and the **maturity date**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Review and deposit

Check the quote, then click **Lock Fixed Rate** and approve in your wallet. It is one signature.
The vault does not need a PT trustline.
</Step>

<Step>
### Hold your receipt

You receive a **receipt**, shown under **Your fixed-rate receipts**. Hold it until maturity, then
click **Redeem** for the full payout in USDC.
</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout title="Your return is locked">
  From this moment your payout is fixed. Market rates can move all they like. Your receipt
  pays out the same amount at maturity regardless, subject to the protocol's risks (see
  [Understanding the risks](/docs/resources/security)).
</Callout>

If the button reads **Not accepting deposits**, the vault has no spare capacity to back a coupon
right now, or the series cap is reached. See
the FAQ entry "Why did a series stop accepting deposits?" in the [FAQ](/docs/faq).

## Prefer to hold the tokens directly?

If you want PT and YT in your own wallet (to trade, to provide liquidity, or to claim yield
yourself), use the **Deposit** page. It supplies your USDC at par and mints equal amounts of **PT** (your fixed
bond) and **YT** (the yield claim).

1. Open **Deposit** under **Earn**.
2. Enter the USDC amount. The two **You receive** boxes show equal PT and YT.
3. If asked, click **Enable PT** (one signature, no USDC moves). See [Trustlines](/docs/getting-started/trustlines).
4. Click **Deposit & Mint**. The app runs two signatures, shown in the **How your funds move** strip: USDC is wrapped into SR through Blend, then the SR is split into PT and YT.

This is the more advanced path. See [How It Works](/docs/how-it-works) and the
[guides](/docs/guides) to decide what to do with each token.

## What happens after you deposit?

| Product | What you hold | What you do later |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Fixed-Rate Vault** | A receipt for a fixed payout | Redeem the receipt at maturity for USDC. |
| **Deposit (PT + YT)** | PT in your wallet (with the trustline), and YT | Hold PT to maturity, claim YT yield over time, or trade either on the market. |

## Tracking your position

The app's **Overview** shows your balances and performance, **Activity** lists your on-chain
deposits, claims and redemptions, and the **Solvency** page shows live that the protocol's
backing covers everything it owes.
