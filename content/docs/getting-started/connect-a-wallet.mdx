To use Spield you connect a **Stellar wallet**: a browser extension (or web wallet) that
holds your keys and signs transactions. You stay in control. Spield never has access to your
secret key, and every action needs your approval in the wallet.

## Supported wallets

The app lists these wallets, in this order:

| Wallet | Type | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Freighter** | Browser extension | The most widely used Stellar wallet. A good default. |
| **xBull** | Browser extension | |
| **Rabet** | Browser extension | |
| **Hana** | Browser extension | |
| **LOBSTR** | Browser extension (LOBSTR Signer) | Install the LOBSTR Signer extension. |
| **Albedo** | Web-based | Nothing to install. Signs in a popup window. |

<Callout type="info">
  Don't have one yet? **Freighter** is the simplest place to start. Install it, create a
  wallet, and back up your recovery phrase somewhere safe.
</Callout>

If you build on Spield, [`@spield/sdk/wallets`](/docs/developers/sdk) offers a ready-made wallet
picker with the same choices.

## Connect to Spield

<Steps>

<Step>
### Install a wallet

Add a wallet extension from its official source and create (or import) an account. Write
down your recovery phrase and keep it offline.
</Step>

<Step>
### Open the Spield app

Go to [app.spield.live](https://app.spield.live) and click **Connect Wallet** at the top right.
</Step>

<Step>
### Choose your wallet

Pick your wallet from the list. Wallets the app detects are marked **Installed**. Others show
an **Install** link. Albedo is marked **Web** and always works.
</Step>

<Step>
### Approve the connection

Your wallet pops up asking to connect. Approve it. The app now shows your address and
balances. Your choice is remembered in this browser, so the session restores after a reload.
</Step>

</Steps>

## Make sure you are on the right network

The app at [app.spield.live](https://app.spield.live) runs on the **Stellar public network
(mainnet)**, and your wallet must be on the same network. If it is not, the action button
changes to **Switch your wallet network** and nothing is sent. Most wallets switch
networks from their settings in one click.

Spield also runs on Stellar **testnet**, where you can try everything with free test funds.
The hosted app is mainnet only, so on testnet use the [SDK](/docs/developers/sdk) (testnet is
its default network). A testnet build of the app shows the same screens, with a sidebar badge that
reads **Stellar TESTNET**. See [Funding your wallet](/docs/getting-started/funding-your-wallet)
for how to get test funds.

<Callout type="warn" title="Always verify the URL">
  Before connecting, double-check you are on the official app domain (`app.spield.live`) and
  bookmark it. Wallet phishing usually relies on look-alike URLs. Never enter your recovery
  phrase into any website. A legitimate site only asks you to *approve transactions* in your
  wallet's own popup.
</Callout>

## Disconnecting

Click your address at the top right and choose **Disconnect**. This just forgets the session
in your browser. Your funds and wallet are unaffected. The same menu has **Copy address**.
