Getting started with Spield takes three steps. This page is the map. Each step has its
own short guide.

<div className="fd-steps">

### Connect a wallet

Spield is used through a **Stellar wallet** (a browser extension or a web wallet such as Albedo).
Connect it once and the app remembers you. → [Connect a wallet](/docs/getting-started/connect-a-wallet)

### Fund your wallet with USDC

You deposit **USDC** into Spield. Get it onto Stellar from an exchange, or bring it from
another chain with Circle CCTP. → [Funding your wallet](/docs/getting-started/funding-your-wallet)

### Make your first deposit

Open [app.spield.live](https://app.spield.live), **pick a series** in the header, then choose what you
want: a **fixed return**, **leveraged yield**, or to **provide liquidity**. The first time you
receive PT in a series you click **Enable PT** once (see [Trustlines](/docs/getting-started/trustlines)).
→ [Your first deposit](/docs/getting-started/your-first-deposit)

</div>

## What you will need

| Requirement | Details |
| --- | --- |
| A **Stellar wallet** | A browser extension like Freighter (see the [full list](/docs/getting-started/connect-a-wallet)). |
| A little **XLM** | Stellar's native asset, used for tiny network fees and account reserves. A few XLM is plenty. |
| Some **USDC** | The asset you deposit. Start with an amount you are comfortable with. |
| An **open series** | Each maturity is a separate series. Check the series menu in the app header. |

## Where things stand today

<Callout title="Three mainnet series are open">
  Spield has been live on Stellar mainnet since September 2026. On 27 November 2026 three new
  series opened: **Blend USDC Series 3** (30 days), **USTRY Series 1** (30 days) and **USTRY Series
  2** (60 days). Blend Series 1 and 2 have matured; you can still
  [redeem those positions](/docs/guides/redeem-at-maturity). To practice without real funds, use
  **testnet**, where Blend and USTRY series are open too. See
  [Series and maturities](/docs/how-it-works/series-and-maturities).
</Callout>
