To use Spield you need two things in your wallet:

- **USDC**, the asset you deposit. On mainnet this is Circle's official USD-backed stablecoin, native on Stellar.
- **A little XLM**, Stellar's native asset. It pays tiny network fees and the small reserve each account and trustline holds.

## USDC asset addresses

If you want to add the USDC asset to your wallet by hand, or check that you hold the right
token, these are the addresses. Spield uses 7-decimal Stellar USDC.

| Network | Asset | Address |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Mainnet** | Circle USDC, classic asset (for trustlines and sends) | `USDC:GA5ZSEJYB37JRC5AVCIA5MOP4RHTM335X2KGX3IHOJAPP5RE34K4KZVN` |
| **Mainnet** | Circle USDC, contract (SAC) | `CCW67TSZV3SSS2HXMBQ5JFGCKJNXKZM7UQUWUZPUTHXSTZLEO7SJMI75` |
| **Testnet** | Blend test USDC, classic asset (used by Blend testnet series) | `USDC:GATALTGTWIOT6BUDBCZM3Q4OQ4BO2COLOAZ7IYSKPLC2PMSOPPGF5V56` |
| **Testnet** | Blend test USDC, contract (SAC) | `CAQCFVLOBK5GIULPNZRGATJJMIZL5BSP7X5YJVMGCPTUEPFM4AVSRCJU` |

<Callout type="warn" title="Testnet has two different test USDC assets">
  **Blend's test USDC** (issuer `GATAL...`) is what the Blend testnet series use. **Circle's test
  USDC** (issuer `GBBD47...`) is a different asset: it is what the Stellar DEX trades against the
  USTRY testnet token and what Circle CCTP delivers on testnet. They are not interchangeable.
  Where the app has to convert between them (for example when you bridge in), it does so for
  you automatically.
</Callout>

## Getting USDC onto Stellar (mainnet)

There are two easy paths. Pick whichever fits where your funds are now.

<Tabs items={['From an exchange', 'From another chain']}>

<Tab value="From an exchange">

Many exchanges support **withdrawing USDC directly on the Stellar network**.

1. On the exchange, choose to withdraw **USDC**.
2. Select **Stellar** as the network (sometimes labelled "XLM network" or "Stellar Lumens").
3. Paste your Stellar wallet address as the destination. Your wallet needs a USDC trustline first.
4. Confirm and wait. Stellar settles in seconds.

You can buy a small amount of **XLM** the same way for fees.

<Callout type="warn" title="Pick the Stellar network">
  When withdrawing, you must choose the **Stellar** network specifically. Sending USDC on
  the wrong network (for example Ethereum) will not arrive in your Stellar wallet.
</Callout>

</Tab>

<Tab value="From another chain">

If your USDC is on **Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum, OP Mainnet, Polygon, Avalanche, or Solana**,
you can bring it to Stellar without an exchange. The app uses **Circle CCTP V2**: your USDC
is burned on the source chain, Circle attests the burn, and native USDC is minted to your
Stellar wallet.

1. Open the **Bridge** page in the Spield app.
2. Choose the **source chain** and connect that chain's wallet.
3. Enter the amount of USDC, review the quote, and confirm.
4. Native Stellar USDC arrives in your wallet, ready to deposit.

You can also pay **directly from another chain** on the Vault, SR Wrapper, Liquidity, Deposit,
and Markets panels: pick the chain in the **Pay with** menu and the app runs the bridge and
the deposit as one resumable journey. See [Bridge USDC](/docs/guides/bridge-usdc) and
[Cross-chain bridge](/docs/features/cross-chain-bridge).

<Callout type="info">
  Cross-chain only moves funds into (or out of) your own Stellar wallet. Once your USDC is
  here, everything Spield does is native. There is no ongoing bridge dependency on your position.
</Callout>

</Tab>

</Tabs>

## Test funds on testnet

Testnet is for trying things out with no real money. Switch your wallet to the Stellar test
network, then:

| You need | How |
| --- | --- |
| **XLM** | Friendbot: `https://friendbot.stellar.org/?addr=YOUR_ADDRESS` gives 10,000 test XLM. |
| **Blend test USDC**, for Blend series | Add the trustline for `USDC:GATALTGTWIOT6BUDBCZM3Q4OQ4BO2COLOAZ7IYSKPLC2PMSOPPGF5V56`, then use the faucet in Blend's testnet app at `testnet.blend.capital`. |
| **USTRY**, for USTRY series | Add the `USTRY:GC3CW7EDYRTWQ635VDIGY6S4ZUF5L6TQ7AA4MWS7LEQDBLUSZXV7UPS4` trustline and buy it on the Stellar DEX. For example, 100 XLM bought about 60 USTRY when the SDK docs were written. |
| **Test USDC from another chain** | The Bridge page accepts test USDC from CCTP testnet chains. For a Blend series the app swaps Circle's test USDC 1:1 into Blend test USDC for you (with at least 99% of the amount guaranteed). |

Testnet pools are small. Keep test amounts to a few units. The full USTRY walkthrough is in
[Treasury yield (USTRY on testnet)](/docs/guides/treasury-yield).

## How much should I start with?

There is no minimum beyond covering fees. A sensible first run:

| Asset | Suggested starter amount |
| --- | --- |
| XLM | A few XLM. An account holds 1 XLM minimum plus 0.5 XLM per trustline, and each transaction costs a fraction of a cent. |
| USDC | Whatever you are comfortable experimenting with. Series have small launch caps. |

Once funded, set up your [trustlines](/docs/getting-started/trustlines) and make
[your first deposit](/docs/getting-started/your-first-deposit).
