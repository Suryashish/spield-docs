Every term you will meet in these docs, defined simply, with links to where each is used in context.

### AMM (Automated Market Maker)
A smart contract that lets people trade against a pool of assets at an algorithmic price, instead of matching
buyers and sellers directly. Spield's [market](/docs/features/yield-market) is a PT/SR time-decay AMM.

### Backing
The live value of the assets standing behind PT and YT. Spield keeps backing at or above everything it owes.
See [Solvency](/docs/how-it-works/solvency).

### Base units
The integer representation of a token amount on-chain. Spield's tokens use 7 decimals, so `10_000_000` base
units equal `1.0`. See [Tokens and units](/docs/developers/tokens).

### Blend
Stellar's primary lending market, where Spield's Blend series source their yield by supplying USDC. See
[The yield source](/docs/how-it-works/yield-source).

### CCTP (Cross-Chain Transfer Protocol)
Circle's protocol for moving native USDC between chains by burning it on one chain and minting it on another.
Spield's cross-chain flows use **CCTP V2**. See [Cross-chain bridge](/docs/features/cross-chain-bridge).

### Claim token
A SEP-41 contract token that a USTRY series issues for a position: the **sUSTRY** receipt, **PT**, and **YT**.
The series contract holds the balances and the token contracts are a facade over them. Because they are
contract tokens, they need no trustline. See [Treasury series (USTRY)](/docs/how-it-works/treasury-series).

### Combine
Burning equal PT and YT before maturity to get the underlying back at par. The Deposit page's **Combine** mode
does this. After maturity PT alone redeems instead.

### Coupon
The fixed extra amount a Fixed-Rate Vault deposit earns on top of principal, the "interest" part of your fixed
payout.

### Discount
How far below par (1.0) a PT trades today. The discount is the source of PT's fixed return.

### Etherfuse
The issuer of **USTRY**, a tokenized US Treasury product on Stellar. See [Treasury series (USTRY)](/docs/how-it-works/treasury-series).

### Finalize
The one-time step after a USTRY series matures that freezes PT's and YT's USTRY allocation. Anyone can do it, once a
post-maturity NAV sample exists or the grace period has passed. PT redeems after finalization.

### Fixed rate
The annualized return you lock in by buying PT (or depositing in the Fixed-Rate Vault) and holding to maturity. It
does not change after you commit.

### Fixed-Rate Vault
Deposit USDC and receive a receipt for a fixed payout at maturity. The vault holds the PT itself, so you need no
trustline. The receipt is an on-chain record under your address, not a transferable token. See
[Fixed-Rate Vault](/docs/features/fixed-rate-vault).

### Guarded Launch Cap
The per-series ceiling on total SR deposits, kept small while a series is new. It gates new deposits only. Redemptions and
exits never check it. Also called the series' deposit cap.

### High-water mark
A value that only ever goes up. SR's exchange rate is clamped to its all-time high, and a USTRY series' NAV
high-water mark rises by at most a capped amount per year. YT earns only when NAV rises above its previous
high-water mark.

### Implied rate (implied APY)
The market's *current* expectation of future yield, read off the PT price in the PT/SR pool. Buying YT is a bet that
**realized** yield will beat the implied rate.

### LP / LP shares
A liquidity provider supplies PT and SR to the market. **LP shares** are your proportional claim on the pool and its
fees, tracked by the market contract. The LP side of every swap fee is 80%.

### Maturity
The fixed date when PT becomes redeemable 1:1 and YT stops earning. It is set once per series and never changes.
See [Series and maturities](/docs/how-it-works/series-and-maturities).

### Multisig (2-of-3)
Spield's admin is a Stellar account with three signers of equal weight and a threshold of 2, so no single key can act
alone. See [Understanding the risks](/docs/resources/security).

### NAV (net asset value)
The value of one unit of an asset such as USTRY, as reported by a price feed. A USTRY series folds the NAV into its
accounting to decide how much of the USTRY belongs to PT and how much to YT.

### Par
The face value of a PT, exactly 1 USDC. PT prices drift up toward par as maturity nears.

### Protocol fees
Spield's yield fee (5% of YT yield, ceiling 10%) and the treasury's share of swap fees (20%, ceiling 50%). See
[Protocol fees](/docs/features/protocol-fees).

### PT (Principal Token)
The fixed-rate bond leg. It redeems 1:1 for the underlying at maturity and is bought at a discount today. A PT is a classic Stellar
asset (code `SPLDPT` on mainnet) with its own issuer per series. See [PT and YT explained](/docs/how-it-works/pt-and-yt).

### Realized APY / yield
The yield that actually accrued, as opposed to the *implied* expectation.

### Redeem
To exchange a token for what it is owed: PT for USDC at maturity, or a vault receipt for its full payout.

### RedStone
The oracle provider whose SEP-40 feed prices USTRY NAV on mainnet.

### Router
A convenience contract (`srrouter`) that lets you pay and receive in plain USDC. It wraps USDC into SR (or unwraps), trades, and returns any
change in one transaction. You can still trade directly in SR without it. See [Trade with SR](/docs/guides/trade-with-sr).

### SAC (Stellar Asset Contract)
The smart-contract wrapper that lets a classic Stellar asset be used by Soroban contracts. PT and USDC are used through SACs. YT is not: the
yield contract is itself the YT token. See [Tokens and units](/docs/developers/tokens).

### SEP-40
The Stellar standard interface for price oracles. Spield's USTRY series reads NAV through it, so any compliant feed can be used.

### SEP-41
The Stellar standard interface for fungible tokens on Soroban. SR, YT, and the USTRY claim tokens implement it.

### Series
One maturity, deployed as its own set of six contracts (SR, strategy, yield engine, market, vault, router) with its
own PT asset and an immutable expiry. See [Series and maturities](/docs/how-it-works/series-and-maturities).

### Slippage
The difference between a quoted price and the executed price on a trade. You set a tolerance so trades only execute
within an acceptable range.

### Solvency
The property that what the protocol holds covers everything it owes. Spield asserts it on every state change and
lets you [verify it live](/docs/features/solvency-dashboard).

### Soroban
Stellar's smart-contract platform, on which Spield's contracts run.

### SR (Standardized Return)
Spield's yield-bearing share token over the yield source, the counterpart of Pendle's SY. Your SR balance stays flat and each SR is worth more
USDC over time. See [Standardized Return (SR)](/docs/how-it-works/standardized-return).

### Stellar
The fast, low-fee blockchain Spield is built on. See [Why Stellar?](/docs/introduction/why-stellar).

### Stripping (yield stripping)
Splitting a yield-bearing position into a fixed leg (PT) and a variable leg (YT). The core model behind Spield.

### sUSTRY
The unsplit receipt for USTRY deposited into a USTRY series. One sUSTRY holds one USTRY, and it can be withdrawn at any time without a live
NAV. It can also be split into PT and YT.

### Trustline
A Stellar account's declaration that it is willing to hold a particular classic asset. PT needs one **per series**. YT and SR do not. See
[Trustlines](/docs/getting-started/trustlines).

### USDC
Circle's USD-backed stablecoin, native on Stellar. The asset Blend series take in and PT redeems into.

### USTRY
Etherfuse's tokenized US Treasury token. The deposit asset of Spield's USTRY series, currently on testnet.

### Vault rate
The fixed rate the Fixed-Rate Vault pays, in basis points per year. It is set by the admin, bounded on-chain by a 20% ceiling, and every receipt
must be backed by PT the vault holds. The vault launch process also checks it against Blend's measured supply rate, but the
contract itself does not enforce that check.

### XLM
Stellar's native asset, used to pay small network fees and account reserves.

### YT (Yield Token)
The variable yield leg, a claim on all yield to maturity. Cheap and leveraged, and it can decay to zero. The yield contract itself is the YT token,
and its transfer hook settles interest on every transfer. See [PT and YT explained](/docs/how-it-works/pt-and-yt).
